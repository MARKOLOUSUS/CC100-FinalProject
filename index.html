Growing Agriculture, Sustaining Fisheries

Discover the importance of agriculture and fisheries in providing food,
supporting livelihoods, and building a sustainable future for our communities.

Get to know the people, practices, and natural resources that help feed
the Philippines and how we can protect them for generations to come.

Welcome to Agriculture and Fisheries!

Agriculture and Fisheries play an important role in our everyday lives. They provide essential food, create employment opportunities, and support the growth of our economy. Our goal is to promote awareness, share knowledge, and encourage sustainable practices that help protect our natural resources.

Agriculture in the Philippines is a foundational pillar of the national economy, a major source of livelihood in rural areas, and a key contributor to national food security.

The fisheries sector in the Philippines is a major pillar of the national economy, a primary source of animal protein for Filipinos, and a vital livelihood for over 2 million fishers and coastal residents.
